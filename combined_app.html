      About

      StyleMate combines body and face analysis, a SQLite store, an AI-ranked product catalog, and a Three.js avatar. The heavy models run as optional local services: MiVOLO (age), MediaPipe + ArUco (height), FashionCLIP (tags, search, recommendations) and the VisionFit-AI clothing classifier by Akshit Kandari (cloth analysis of uploaded garments). When a service is off, the app falls back to simpler local logic and tells you so.
